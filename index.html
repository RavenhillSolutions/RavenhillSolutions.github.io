About

About Ravenhill Solutions

Ravenhill Solutions is a Louisiana-based consulting firm founded by Craig Bertrand. The firm combines executive-level governance and compliance experience with hands-on business systems, process architecture, ERP, and digital transformation expertise.

Craig has more than 15 years of experience building and integrating operating systems across industrial services, energy-related organizations, and nonprofit environments. His work has supported ISO certification, enterprise systems modernization, major customer requirements, organizational integration, operational scalability, and acquisition-related transformation.
